Digital Bingo Board

A digital bingo switchboard. Run the game from your phone; show the board on the TV.

Open Display Full-screen board for the TV Open Controller Operator switchboard for your phone or iPad
  1. Open Display on the TV-connected device and go full screen.
  2. Open Controller on your phone and enter the room code shown on the Display.
  3. Draw, call, and run the game from the Controller.
